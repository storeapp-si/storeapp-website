Reach people looking for their next useful software tool

StoreApp.si is an independent discovery directory for AI tools and software. We publish tool overviews, practical guides and task-based comparisons to help visitors make more informed choices.

Partnership formats we can consider

Affiliate partnerships

Where a provider or network approves StoreApp.si, we may use a tracked referral link and clearly disclose the commercial relationship near the link or recommendation.

Editorial product submissions

Providers may submit products for consideration. Inclusion depends on relevance, a verifiable product website and editorial review; submission does not guarantee publication.

Sponsored placements

Clearly labeled sponsorships may be considered when relevant to readers. Payment does not buy a positive review, a guaranteed ranking or editorial control.

What we need from a potential partner

  • Official product and company website.
  • A concise explanation of the product, target users and main use cases.
  • Current public pricing and plan information, if available.
  • Affiliate program terms, commission structure, cookie window and geographic restrictions, if proposing referrals.
  • Brand assets and approved descriptions, where available.

How we evaluate opportunities

  1. Relevance: Does the product solve a real task for the audience?
  2. Verifiability: Can visitors confirm the product, terms and pricing on an official source?
  3. User value: Can we explain who should use it, who should not, and what alternatives exist?
  4. Transparency: Can any commercial relationship be disclosed clearly?

Current stage and transparency

StoreApp.si is an early-stage website. We do not publish unverified traffic, conversion, revenue or audience claims. We do not claim that a provider endorses StoreApp.si unless that relationship is confirmed.

Official provider links are not automatically affiliate links. We will identify compensated links and sponsored placements clearly. Read our commercial disclosure policy.

A partnership inquiry is not an endorsement or a promise of placement. All proposals are subject to review and written approval.
