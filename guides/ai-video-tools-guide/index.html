AI Video Tools: How to Choose a Workflow

AI video is not one category. Some products generate footage, others edit clips, create presenter avatars or produce narration.

1. Generate visual footage

Runway is oriented toward AI-assisted video creation. Check current generation limits, export quality, commercial-use terms and plan restrictions.

2. Edit and publish short videos

CapCut can fit workflows involving trimming, captions, templates and social content.

3. Create presenter-style videos

HeyGen focuses on avatar-presenter and related workflows. Review consent and likeness rules before creating content involving real people.

4. Add narration and audio

ElevenLabs offers voice and audio tools. Confirm rights to any voice, script or source recording.

5. Choose by output and rights

Test a short sample and compare language support, rendering time, watermarks, export formats and commercial rights.

Tools to explore

Runway

A creative platform with AI tools for generating and editing video and supporting visual production workflows.

CapCut

A video editor with templates and AI-assisted features for creating and editing social content.

HeyGen

A platform for creating presenter-style videos with AI avatars and supported video translation features.

ElevenLabs

A voice AI platform offering text-to-speech, voice generation and other audio tools.

Suno

An AI music tool that can generate songs from prompts, including music and vocals in supported workflows.

Plans, features and regional availability can change. Verify official information and review privacy terms before sharing sensitive data.
