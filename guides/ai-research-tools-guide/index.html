AI Research Tools: Search, Sources and Document Study

Research tools are useful when matched to the source material and paired with independent fact-checking.

1. For web discovery

Perplexity is designed around search and answers with source links. Open cited sources and check that they support the claim.

2. For your own documents

NotebookLM helps work with supplied sources and create summaries or study materials. Confirm important statements against the originals.

3. For broad explanations

ChatGPT, Claude and Google Gemini can help explain topics and synthesize notes, but fluent output is not proof.

4. Keep a source trail

Record source URLs, dates, evidence and uncertainties. Separate what a source says from your interpretation.

5. Protect confidential material

Review data-use terms before uploading unpublished research or personal information.

Tools to explore

Perplexity

An AI-powered answer and search tool designed to help people investigate questions and explore information with sources.

NotebookLM

A research assistant that helps you work with supplied sources, organize information and create summaries and study materials.

An AI assistant for writing, brainstorming, learning, problem-solving and working through everyday questions and tasks.
