AI Writing Tools: A Practical Selection Guide

AI writing tools serve different purposes. A general assistant is not the same as proofreading, translation or paraphrasing.

1. For drafting and brainstorming

ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini and Microsoft Copilot can help with outlines and first drafts. Verify claims, names, dates and citations.

2. For proofreading and clarity

Grammarly focuses on feedback such as grammar, spelling, clarity and tone.

3. For paraphrasing and summaries

QuillBot offers rephrasing and summarizing tools. Compare the result with the original so meaning and attribution are preserved.

4. For translation

DeepL and Google Translate focus on translation. For high-stakes content, have a qualified person check terminology and meaning.

5. A responsible workflow

Keep source notes, fact-check generated statements and follow academic or workplace rules for AI assistance.

Tools to explore

ChatGPT

An AI assistant for writing, brainstorming, learning, problem-solving and working through everyday questions and tasks.

Claude

An AI assistant from Anthropic that can help with writing, document analysis, explanations and complex tasks.

Grammarly

A writing assistant that checks grammar, spelling, clarity and tone, with additional AI writing features.

QuillBot

A collection of writing tools for paraphrasing, summarizing and checking text.

DeepL

A translation service with writing tools designed to help users translate and refine text across supported languages.

Google Translate

Google’s translation service for text and supported forms of voice, image and document translation.

Plans, features and regional availability can change. Verify official information and review privacy terms before sharing sensitive data.
