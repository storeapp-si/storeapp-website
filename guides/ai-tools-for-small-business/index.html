How to Choose AI Tools for a Small Business

Small businesses rarely need every new AI product. They need a small set of tools that solve real tasks, fit the team's skills and justify their cost.

1. Start with a task, not a trend

Write down the recurring job first: drafting product copy, summarizing documents, creating social graphics or connecting software. Define the time currently spent and what a useful result looks like.

2. Build a simple starter stack

General assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini or Microsoft Copilot can support drafting. Canva can help with visual content, while Zapier may connect repetitive workflows. Pick tools that fit your process.

3. Check privacy and review requirements

Do not paste confidential information into a service until you have reviewed its data-use terms, security controls and your own obligations. Human review remains important for customer-facing and business-critical work.

4. Test cost and workflow fit

Check current plan limits, export options, collaboration features and cancellation terms on official sites. Run a short trial with a real task before committing.

5. Measure useful outcomes

Track time saved, output quality, rework, adoption and total cost. A tool that is not used consistently may not be worth keeping.

Tools to explore

ChatGPT

An AI assistant for writing, brainstorming, learning, problem-solving and working through everyday questions and tasks.

Claude

An AI assistant from Anthropic that can help with writing, document analysis, explanations and complex tasks.

Google Gemini

Google’s AI assistant for writing, exploring ideas, working with supported files and images, and tackling everyday questions.

Microsoft Copilot

Microsoft’s AI assistant for answering questions, drafting content and supporting everyday work.

Canva

A visual design platform with templates and AI-assisted features for creating graphics, presentations and other content.

Zapier

An automation platform that connects supported apps to reduce repetitive tasks and build workflows.

Plans, features and regional availability can change. Verify official information and review privacy terms before sharing sensitive data.
