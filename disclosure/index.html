Commercial disclosure and affiliate policy

StoreApp.si aims to make software discovery useful and transparent. Commercial relationships must never be confused with independent editorial information.

Affiliate links

If StoreApp.si joins an affiliate or referral program and publishes a tracked link, we will clearly identify it as an affiliate link near the relevant recommendation or link. If a visitor makes a qualifying purchase or signs up through that link, StoreApp.si may earn a commission at no additional cost to the visitor, subject to the program's terms.

A link to a provider's official website is not, by itself, an affiliate link. We will not describe a link as an affiliate link unless it is actually tracked under an approved program.

Sponsored placements

Paid placements, sponsored articles or other paid promotions will be labeled clearly. Payment does not guarantee a positive assessment, inclusion in an editorial comparison or a particular ranking.

Editorial independence

  • Products are considered for relevance, usefulness and verifiable information.
  • Commercial relationships do not determine our editorial conclusions.
  • We aim to explain limitations and suitable alternatives, not only benefits.
  • We do not invent product tests, user reviews, traffic statistics, prices, endorsements or partnership approvals.

How comparisons are prepared

Our comparisons may use public provider information and task-based decision criteria. Unless a page explicitly describes a documented hands-on test, do not interpret it as a controlled benchmark or a claim that we tested every feature. Readers should verify current features, pricing, availability, terms and data policies with the provider.

Questions and corrections

For a correction or a commercial inquiry, visit our partnerships page or use the tool submission page.

This policy is intended to explain our approach in plain language. It is not legal advice and does not replace any disclosures required by applicable law or an affiliate program.
