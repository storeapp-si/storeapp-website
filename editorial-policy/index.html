Editorial policy and review methodology

StoreApp.si is an independent directory that helps people discover AI tools and make more informed software choices. This page explains how we select products, prepare editorial content and handle commercial relationships.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Tool selection

We consider tools for inclusion when they have a verifiable official website, a clear product purpose and a relevant use case for people exploring AI software. We consider relevance, practical usefulness, product maturity, audience fit and the quality of publicly available information. A submission does not guarantee inclusion.

We aim to identify whether a product is a standalone service, a feature within a larger platform, a research project or an enterprise solution. We avoid presenting a feature as a separate product when that distinction would mislead visitors.

2. Research and source checking

We prioritize primary sources: official product pages, documentation, pricing pages, terms, privacy notices, release notes and support materials. Independent sources may be used to add context where appropriate. We check important claims against available sources and avoid publishing unsupported specifications, customer counts, performance claims or partnership statements.

Product features, prices, plan limits, regional availability and terms change frequently. Where we cannot verify a current detail, we say so or direct readers to the provider rather than guessing.

3. Tool-page standards

Tool pages are intended to explain what a product does, common use cases, who may find it useful, relevant limitations and what visitors should verify before adopting it. We aim to distinguish provider-described features from our own analysis. A listing is not an endorsement, guarantee or claim that every feature has been independently tested.

We do not claim hands-on testing unless the page describes the test actually performed. We do not invent user reviews, ratings, benchmark results, prices, discounts or personal experience.

4. Comparisons and practical tests

Comparisons focus on decision factors such as task fit, workflow, integrations, output quality, limitations, privacy, availability and total cost. A decision guide is not a controlled benchmark. When we conduct a practical test, we should disclose the test date, product versions or plans where known, input or prompt, evaluation criteria, observed results and limitations so readers can understand what was and was not tested.

We do not declare a universal winner when the result depends on a visitor's task, region, account type or requirements. Readers should verify important outputs and current provider terms independently.

5. Sensitive and high-impact categories

For health, legal, financial, employment, education and security-related products, we take particular care to describe intended use and limitations. AI tools should not be presented as a substitute for qualified professional judgment, required human review or applicable safety procedures. Security tools should be used only in systems the user is authorized to assess.

6. Corrections and updates

We welcome specific correction requests, especially when accompanied by a reliable primary source. To report an error, use the Contact page and include the StoreApp.si page URL, the claim in question and the evidence supporting the correction. We review reported issues and update pages when we can verify the change. We may revise or remove listings that become unavailable, misleading or no longer relevant.

7. Affiliate links, sponsorships and independence

StoreApp.si may earn revenue through approved affiliate or referral links and clearly labeled sponsorships. Commercial links will be identified near the relevant link or recommendation. Payment does not guarantee inclusion, a positive assessment, a particular ranking or editorial control. Official provider links are not automatically affiliate links.

Our commercial approach is explained in the Commercial Disclosure. Editorial conclusions should remain based on the stated criteria and available evidence, not on whether a provider pays us.

8. Use of AI in producing content

AI tools may assist with research organization, drafting or editing. They do not replace source verification or editorial responsibility. We are responsible for the content we publish and aim to correct material errors when identified.

9. Contact

Questions about our selection criteria, a correction or an editorial concern can be sent through the Contact page.
