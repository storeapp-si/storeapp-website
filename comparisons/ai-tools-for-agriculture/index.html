AI in agriculture: crop diagnosis, precision spraying and farm analytics

Plantix, John Deere See & Spray and Climate FieldView are not direct substitutes. One supports crop problem identification, another targets weed detection and selective spraying, while a farm data platform helps organise field-level information. The right choice follows the decision you need to improve.

Editorial method: This is a task-based decision guide, not a controlled benchmark or universal ranking. Product capabilities, access and pricing can change. Confirm current details with each vendor and test on your own representative workflow.

The short answer

Plantix

Evaluate for image-assisted crop problem identification and farmer-facing agronomy guidance; confirm crop, region and disease coverage.

John Deere See & Spray

Evaluate for machine-based weed detection and targeted application in compatible equipment and operating conditions.

Climate FieldView

Evaluate for collecting, viewing and analysing field and machine data to support farm management decisions.

Compare by field decision

Crop issue identification

Test images of known cases across crop stages, lighting and local varieties. Track false positives, false negatives and escalation to an agronomist.

Targeted spraying

Check supported machines, crops, weed types, speed, environmental conditions, chemical compatibility and local regulatory requirements.

Field analytics

Check data sources, machine compatibility, connectivity, map quality, export options and whether insights change a specific operational decision.

Practical pilot

  • Run a small, clearly bounded pilot across representative fields and document crop, region, weather and operating conditions.
  • Compare AI-assisted recommendations with agronomist assessment or established field scouting.
  • Measure outcomes that matter: scouting time, missed detections, input use, yield quality and total operating cost.
  • Confirm local support, equipment availability, data ownership and subscription terms before scaling.

Important limitation

  • Image-based suggestions are not a definitive diagnosis, and automated spraying depends on machine configuration and local rules. Follow product labels, agronomic advice and applicable pesticide and environmental regulations.
