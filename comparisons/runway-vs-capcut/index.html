Runway vs CapCut: AI Video Creation or Editing?

Runway and CapCut can serve different stages: generating or transforming visuals versus editing and packaging clips.

Neutral decision framework, not a claim that one product is universally better. Verify current features and pricing with providers.

What to comparePractical check
Generated visualsTest Runway's current generation and editing features against a short visual brief.
Existing footageCapCut may fit workflows centered on trimming, captions, templates and social-ready edits.
Publishing and exportCompare resolution, export controls, watermarks and formats.
Rights and costCheck current terms, commercial rights and usage restrictions before client work.

A simple decision process

  1. Choose one real task and define the required output.
  2. Run the same small test in each product.
  3. Check correctness, time saved, editing effort and workflow fit.
  4. Review pricing, privacy, terms and regional availability.
