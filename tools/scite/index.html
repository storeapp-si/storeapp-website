Scite: AI tool overview and use cases

A research platform that analyses citation contexts to help users see whether later papers support, contrast with or mention a scholarly claim.

Category: Research · Citation analysis

Useful for: Checking citation context and assessing how research claims are discussed

Official website: scite.ai ↗

What is Scite?

Scite provides citation-context information to help researchers understand how publications refer to earlier work. Citation classifications are useful signals, not a complete quality assessment; inspect the citing papers and their context.

Best-fit workflows

Search a paper or claim, inspect supporting and contrasting citation statements, and read the citing articles to understand why the claim is referenced.

Limitations and points to verify

Citation context does not automatically determine whether a study is methodologically sound or whether a claim is true. Read the cited and citing papers.

Who may find it useful?

Researchers, postgraduate students and librarians. Suitability depends on the user's discipline, access, budget and workflow.
