Consensus: overview and use cases

An AI-assisted research search engine designed to help explore findings from scientific papers and academic literature.

Useful for: Finding and reviewing academic research

Official website: consensus.app ↗

Visit official website ↗Browse all tools

What is Consensus?

Consensus helps users search scientific literature and explore research findings. It can make discovery faster, but it does not replace reading the original papers or evaluating study quality.

When might it help?

Use it to identify relevant papers, compare reported findings and build a starting bibliography.

What should you consider?

Limitations

Search coverage and summaries can be incomplete. A paper's abstract or AI summary is not a substitute for methods, limitations and full-text review.

Evaluation

Search a question you know well, open several cited papers and verify that summaries accurately reflect the results and limitations.

Before choosing

Check database coverage, full-text access, citation details, usage limits and privacy terms.

Best-fit workflows

Starting a literature review, comparing study findings and locating papers relevant to a research question.

When another option may fit better

Consider another product if your workflow requires a different specialist capability, stricter control over data or output, or integrations this service does not support.
