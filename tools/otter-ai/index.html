Otter.ai: overview and use cases

An AI meeting assistant that transcribes conversations and helps create searchable notes, summaries and action items.

What is Otter.ai?

Otter.ai provides speech transcription and meeting-focused AI features. Its usefulness depends on audio quality, speaker clarity, supported languages and meeting permissions.

When might it help?

Helpful for teams that need searchable meeting records and a first draft of decisions or follow-up tasks.

What should you consider?

Limitations

Transcripts can mishear names, technical terms and overlapping speech. Recording meetings may require consent and organizational approval.

Evaluation

Test it on a short meeting with clear consent, then compare the transcript and action items against the recording.

Before choosing

Review supported meeting platforms and languages, recording consent requirements, retention controls, integrations and plan limits.

Best-fit workflows

Capturing meeting discussions and turning spoken conversations into notes that can be reviewed after the call.

When another option may fit better

Consider another product if your workflow requires a different specialist capability, stricter control over data or output, or integrations this service does not support.
