ResearchRabbit: AI tool overview and use cases

A literature-discovery and mapping tool that helps researchers explore connected papers, authors and research networks from an initial set of studies.

What is ResearchRabbit?

ResearchRabbit helps researchers discover related papers and authors through literature networks. It is particularly useful after identifying a few relevant seed papers, though coverage depends on available metadata and indexing.

Best-fit workflows

Add a small set of high-quality seed papers, explore related work and authors, then screen the resulting papers for relevance and quality.

Limitations and points to verify

Discovery depends on indexed scholarly metadata and the seed papers selected. It is a discovery aid, not a systematic-review database or completeness guarantee.

Who may find it useful?

University students and academic researchers. Suitability depends on the user's discipline, access, budget and workflow.

How to evaluate it

Test it with a representative task whose correct result you can independently check. Compare accuracy, time saved, explainability, export quality and total cost.

Before choosing

Review current pricing, regional availability, data retention, privacy terms, integrations, licensing and the provider's documentation.

Responsible use: Treat AI-generated outputs as drafts or decision-support material. Verify technical assumptions, source data, licensing and project-specific requirements before relying on them in professional or academic work.
