Satellite Imagery Platforms

A practical starting point for viewing, comparing and downloading satellite imagery and Earth-observation data. The resources below include free public-data services; coverage, resolution, available dates and download conditions vary.

Use it to explore Sentinel satellite imagery, compare dates, inspect different visualisations and download available data. A free account may be required for some features.

Choosing the right source

  • Recent optical imagery: start with Copernicus Browser and inspect Sentinel-2 acquisition dates and cloud cover.
  • Historical comparisons: use USGS EarthExplorer to search the Landsat archive.
  • Global visual monitoring: use NASA Worldview for broad-scale imagery and time-enabled layers.
  • Landsat documentation and access: consult the official USGS Landsat Data Access page.
Important limitation: Satellite imagery can help identify surface patterns and changes, but it cannot by itself confirm the presence of gold, gemstones, ore deposits or other underground resources. Ground surveys, geological maps, sampling and qualified interpretation are required.
