Descript: overview and use cases

An audio and video editor that lets creators edit recordings through a text transcript, with AI-assisted production features.

Useful for: Podcast editing, spoken-word video and transcript-based editing

Official website: descript.com ↗

Visit official website ↗Browse all tools

What is Descript?

Descript combines media editing with transcript-based workflows and AI-assisted tools. It is designed to make dialogue-heavy editing more approachable.

When might it help?

Useful when the content is primarily speech and editing the transcript is faster than working directly with a traditional timeline.

What should you consider?

Limitations

Transcript errors can cause awkward cuts, and complex visual edits may still be easier in a conventional video editor.

Evaluation

Edit a short interview, check cut accuracy, audio transitions, captions and export quality before moving a full project.

Before choosing

Check supported media formats, transcription limits, export restrictions, collaboration features and rights for generated assets.

Best-fit workflows

Editing interviews, podcasts and spoken-word videos by removing or rearranging transcript sections.

When another option may fit better

Consider another product if your workflow requires a different specialist capability, stricter control over data or output, or integrations this service does not support.
