Privacy notice

This notice explains the information StoreApp.si processes when visitors browse the directory, including the use of Google Analytics 4 to measure website activity.

Information collected through website analytics

StoreApp.si uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a measurement service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content and performance. Depending on the visitor\u2019s browser and settings, GA4 may process information such as pages viewed, interactions, approximate location, device and browser information, referral source and online identifiers or cookies. Analytics reports are used to understand aggregated website activity; StoreApp.si does not ask visitors to submit sensitive personal information through analytics.

Google processes data under its own terms and privacy policies. Learn more in Google\u2019s Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data safeguards.

Cookies and browser controls

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to distinguish visits and measure interactions. Visitors can manage or delete cookies through their browser settings and may use Google\u2019s available controls for analytics. Blocking or deleting cookies can affect measurement. StoreApp.si does not currently require visitors to create an account to browse the directory.

Search and filters

Search and category filters run in the browser. A shared search term may appear in the URL and browser history. Do not include sensitive personal information in search terms or links.

Third-party websites and external services

Tool listings link to third-party providers. The suggestion workflow may open a GitHub issue draft for the visitor to review and submit. When visitors follow an external link or submit information to another service, that provider\u2019s privacy policy and data practices apply. StoreApp.si does not control those services.

Advertising and affiliate links

StoreApp.si will identify affiliate links and sponsored placements clearly when they are introduced. Advertising services, including Google AdSense, may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize ads if advertising is enabled in the future. This notice will be updated when such services are activated. Visitors should review the relevant provider policies and available ad-personalization controls.

Data retention and choices

Analytics data retention is managed through the Google Analytics property settings and Google\u2019s applicable policies. Visitors who do not want analytics cookies can adjust browser settings or use available Google opt-out controls. Some measurement information may still be processed in a limited form depending on browser and consent settings.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact StoreApp.si through the contact page. This notice may be updated when the website adds or changes analytics, advertising, affiliate, hosting or other services. The notice is provided for general information and is not legal advice.
