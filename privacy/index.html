Privacy notice

This notice explains how StoreApp.si handles information when you visit the website, use its directory features or follow links to other services.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Service status: StoreApp.si uses Google Analytics 4 for website measurement. Google AdSense advertising is not currently enabled on the website. This notice will be revised before advertising is activated, including any required consent and privacy controls.

1. Who operates this website

StoreApp.si operates an independent directory of AI tools and software. For privacy questions or requests relating to this website, use the Contact page. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information by ordinary email.

2. Information processed when you visit

When you access a website, the hosting, delivery and security services used to operate it may process technical information such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, timestamps and security events. These records help deliver the website, protect it against abuse and troubleshoot availability. Retention and processing depend on the relevant service provider and its settings.

StoreApp.si does not currently require an account to browse the directory. Search and category filtering are performed in your browser. Search terms may be reflected in the page URL or browser history if you share or revisit that URL; avoid putting sensitive information into search fields.

3. Google Analytics 4

StoreApp.si uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content and performance. Depending on configuration, browser settings and applicable consent choices, analytics may process information such as pages and events viewed, approximate location, device and browser information, referral source, identifiers and cookie-related data.

Analytics reports are used to understand website activity. Google processes information under its own terms and policies. Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics safeguards. You can also review Google's information about the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, where available.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. Google Analytics and other services may use cookies or similar technologies for measurement, remembering settings, security or other functions. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website functions or the accuracy of analytics.

Where consent is required by applicable law, non-essential cookies and similar technologies should only be used after the required choice has been obtained. StoreApp.si will configure and publish the appropriate consent controls before enabling advertising or other consent-dependent technologies in relevant regions.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

As of the date above, Google AdSense ads are not enabled on StoreApp.si. If advertising is introduced, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, measure, limit or personalize ads, subject to the applicable settings, consent choices and Google policies.

Before ads are activated, this notice and the site's consent experience will be updated to explain the advertising technologies actually used and provide the choices required for visitors in applicable regions. Visitors can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at Google's partner sites policy and manage relevant ad settings through My Ad Center, where available.

6. External websites, tools and submissions

Directory listings link to third-party AI products and services. The provider's own privacy notice and terms apply when you visit those websites, create an account or submit information to them. StoreApp.si does not control their data practices.

The tool-suggestion and partnership workflows may open a draft on GitHub for you to review and submit. If you submit an issue, GitHub may process and display the information according to its own terms; public issue content may be visible to others. Do not include confidential or sensitive information.

7. Affiliate links and sponsored content

Some links may become affiliate links if StoreApp.si is accepted into a relevant program and adds a tracked link. Such links and paid placements will be clearly identified. A normal link to a provider's official website is not automatically an affiliate link. See our Commercial Disclosure for details.

8. Data sharing and retention

StoreApp.si does not sell visitors' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, security, analytics or website operations, subject to their roles and applicable terms. StoreApp.si does not operate a visitor account database or a direct-submission database at this time.

Retention periods depend on the relevant provider and configuration. GA4 retention is managed through the Analytics property settings. Hosting and security logs are retained according to the applicable provider's policies and settings.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

You can control cookies through your browser and use the privacy or advertising controls made available by the relevant provider. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information. To ask a question or make a request relating to StoreApp.si, use the Contact page. We may need enough information to understand and verify a request; do not send identity documents or sensitive data unless specifically necessary and requested through an appropriate channel.

10. Children's privacy

StoreApp.si is a general software directory and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of third-party services and review the providers' age requirements and privacy terms.

11. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website changes its analytics, advertising, hosting, submission or other services. The latest revision date appears near the top of this page. The notice is intended as a plain-language explanation and is not legal advice.
