Frequently asked questions

Learn how to use the directory and what to check before choosing an AI tool.

What is StoreApp.si?

StoreApp.si is an independent directory designed to help people discover AI tools across areas such as writing, research, design, video creation, coding and productivity.

How do I find a specific AI tool?

Use the search box to search by tool name, task or keyword. You can also select a category to narrow the listings.

Are the listed tools free?

Pricing varies by provider. Some tools offer free access or trials, while others require payment for certain features. Check each official website for current plans and conditions.

Does StoreApp.si own or operate these tools?

No. The products listed belong to their respective providers. StoreApp.si is an independent discovery directory and is not claiming ownership of the listed products.

How can I check a tool's features and availability?

Open the official website linked on each listing. The provider is the best source for current features, supported countries, account requirements, terms and pricing.

Are the descriptions endorsements or guarantees?

No. The descriptions are brief introductions to help with discovery. Visitors should evaluate each provider and product for themselves before relying on a service or making a purchase.
